Student Of The Year

2025

Co-operative Education Student of the Year, Kasetsart University

I participated in the CWIE Project Competition under the Science and Technology category, which is one of the four categories in the selection process. Among 11 projects in my category, mine was selected as the most outstanding. As a result, I was chosen to represent Kasetsart University in the next round at the Central Regional level.

The Winner Of CWIE (Central–Southern Region)

2025

The CWIE Co-operative Education Competition (Central–Southern Region), Science and Technology Category

At the Central Southern Regional level, I competed against students from 9 universities in the region. The competition involved presenting on a large stage in front of a panel of judges and a full audience. In this round, I was awarded First Prize, earning the opportunity to represent the Central Southern Region in the national-level competition held by the Office of the Permanent Secretary for Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation. This final stage was aimed at selecting the best CWIE project in Thailand.
